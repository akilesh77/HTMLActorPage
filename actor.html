Actor

An actor is a person who portrays a character in a theatrical, film, television, radio, or digital production. Definition and Overview • Role Portrayal: An actor interprets a script and embodies a fictional or real-life person. • Mediums: Performances happen live on stage (theater) or are recorded for media like film, television, and streaming. • Terminology: While "actress" traditionally referred to a female performer, "actor" is now widely used as a gender-neutral term for anyone in the profession

Types of Actors • Character Actor: Specializes in playing unusual, quirky, or distinctive supporting roles rather than leading romantic or action parts. • Method Actor: Uses psychological techniques and personal emotional experiences to deeply connect with and build a character. • Voice Actor: Provides vocal performances for animated films, video games, audiobooks, and commercials without appearing on camera. • Stage Actor: Performs live in front of an audience in theaters, musicals, or operas.
